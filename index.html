COMPANY SPOTLIGHT
Aqueos Corporation
About Aqueos
Aqueos Corporation is all about working on and under the water. They handle marine construction, commercial diving, underwater robots, and vessel services that support industries like oil and gas, civil projects, renewable energy, and offshore wind.
Aqueos Headquarters
Aqueos is based in Louisiana, with additional locations in Texas and California.
Company News
Aqueos just took a big step by officially acquiring the DSV Kelly Ann, a specialized dive support vessel they’ve relied on since 2019. The ship is built for tough underwater work like pipeline installs, inspections, and deepwater construction, and bringing it fully into their fleet helps Aqueos boost its offshore capabilities for the long run.
